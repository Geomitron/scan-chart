The `PART DRUMS` track holds the notes of every difficulty for each pad and the kick, and modifiers that change how those notes are hit:

```text
PART DRUMS
  0      Track name  PART DRUMS
  0      Text        [ENABLE_CHART_DYNAMICS]
  1920   Note on     96   Expert kick
  1920   Note on     98   Expert yellow
  1920   Note off    96
  1920   Note off    98
  2400   Note on     97   Expert red, velocity 127
  2400   Note off    97
  2880   Note on     99   Expert blue
  2880   Note on     111  Blue tom marker
  2880   Note off    99
  2880   Note off    111
```

Here the kick and a yellow cymbal come at tick 1920, then an accented red at tick 2400, then a blue tom at tick 2880.

## Drum types

A drums chart is 4-lane, pro or 5-lane, as [Drum types](/chart/drums#drum-types) describes for a `.chart` file. A scanner decides the type in the same order, from `song.ini` and the drums track, with two differences:

- A tom marker, 110, 111 or 112, makes the chart pro, where a `.chart` file has a cymbal marker.
- A 5-lane green note, 65, 77, 89 or 101, makes it 5-lane, where a `.chart` file has `N 5`.

**Edge case: a tom marker that covers no note.** It changes no note, but it still makes the chart pro, in Clone Hero and in YARG. scan-chart doesn't count such a marker, and its export leaves it out.

**Edge case: YARG's default type.** YARG reads a `.mid` drums chart that has no tom marker and no 5-lane green note as pro, unless `song.ini` has `pro_drums = False`. Every yellow, blue and green note is then a cymbal in YARG. Clone Hero and scan-chart read such a chart as 4-lane.

Playing one type on another kit works as in a `.chart` file, with 100 and 101 in place of `N 4` and `N 5`. See [Playing one type on another kit](/chart/drums#playing-one-type-on-another-kit).

**Edge case: 100 and 101 at one tick in a pro chart.** Clone Hero and scan-chart move 101 to a blue tom, and YARG plays both in the green lane, as a green cymbal and a green tom. An exporter should not write them together in a pro chart.

## Notes

| Note | Easy | Medium | Hard | Expert | `.chart` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Double kick | 59 | 71 | 83 | 95 | `N 32` |
| Kick | 60 | 72 | 84 | 96 | `N 0` |
| Red | 61 | 73 | 85 | 97 | `N 1` |
| Yellow | 62 | 74 | 86 | 98 | `N 2` |
| Blue | 63 | 75 | 87 | 99 | `N 3` |
| Green, or orange in 5-lane | 64 | 76 | 88 | 100 | `N 4` |
| Green tom in a pro chart, or green in 5-lane | 65 | 77 | 89 | 101 | `N 5` |

A drum note's length has no effect.

### Double kicks

Note 95 is a kick that a scanner plays only when the player has the double kick modifier enabled, as `N 32` is in a `.chart` file. See [Double kicks](/chart/drums#double-kicks).

- An exporter should write double kicks only on Expert, as note 95.
- A tick must not hold both 95 and 96.

**Edge case:** Clone Hero plays 59, 71 and 83 as double kicks on Easy, Medium and Hard, when the double kick modifier is on. YARG's song list doesn't count them, and scan-chart reports them as `difficultyForbiddenNote`.

## Modifiers

A drum modifier is a note that changes how the pad notes it covers are hit. It covers the notes of every difficulty that start at or after its note-on and before its note-off, and one of length 0 covers the notes on its tick. Its velocity and channel have no effect.

A `.chart` modifier applies only to the notes at its own tick. See [Modifiers](/chart/drums#modifiers).

### Toms

In a pro chart, a yellow, blue or green note is a cymbal, unless a tom marker covers it. This is the reverse of a `.chart` file, where a cymbal marker makes a cymbal of a tom.

| Note | Makes a tom of |
|---|---|
| `110` | Yellow, 98 on Expert |
| `111` | Blue, 99 on Expert |
| `112` | Green, 100 on Expert |

- Red is always the snare, and 101 is always a tom.
- In a 5-lane chart, tom markers have no effect: each pad is a tom or a cymbal by its lane.
- Tom markers of one color must not overlap.

### Accents and ghosts

An accent is a note hit harder than the ones around it, and a ghost is one hit softer. In a `.mid` file, a pad note's velocity makes it one, after the text event `[ENABLE_CHART_DYNAMICS]`:

| Velocity | Note |
|---|---|
| 127 | Accent |
| 1 | Ghost |
| Any other | A normal note |

- `[ENABLE_CHART_DYNAMICS]` is a text event, `FF 01`, in the drums track. It's written `[ENABLE_CHART_DYNAMICS]`, **Recommended**, or `ENABLE_CHART_DYNAMICS`.
- It covers the notes that come after it in the track, on every difficulty. **Recommended:** at tick 0, before every note.
- Without it, velocity has no effect.
- Every pad note may be an accent or a ghost, 101 included. Kicks have no accents or ghosts. An exporter should not write a kick at velocity 127 or 1 after the event.

**Edge case: kick velocities.** Released Clone Hero reads a kick or double kick at velocity 127 or 1, after the event, as an accent or a ghost. YARG and scan-chart don't.

In a `.chart` file, accents and ghosts are markers, `N 34` to `N 44`, and need no event. See [Accents and ghosts](/chart/drums#accents-and-ghosts).

### Flams

Note 109 makes every drum note it covers a flam: two hits of the pad in quick succession. It's the `.mid` form of `N 109`, and covers a range instead of one tick. See [Flams](/chart/drums#flams).

**Edge case:** YARG doesn't read note 109 in a drums track.

### Disco flip

A `mix` event sets up the drum audio for one difficulty, and its `d` flag turns on disco flip, as in a `.chart` file. See [Disco flip](/chart/drums#disco-flip). In a `.mid` file it's a text event in the drums track:

```text
<tick>  Text  [mix <difficulty> drums<configuration><flag>]
```

- The difficulty in the text, `0` to `3`, picks the difficulty it applies to. In a `.chart` file, the event must also be in that difficulty's section.
- **Recommended:** square brackets, and single spaces between the parts: `[mix 3 drums0d]`.
- An exporter should not write disco flip events in a 5-lane chart.

**Edge case:** a scanner also accepts `_` between the parts, and the text without square brackets.

A flip lasts from its tick, notes on it included, until the event that turns it off, or the end of the track. A `dnoflip` event must not come while a `d` flip of its difficulty is on: turn the flip off first.

## Deprecated

- **`PART DRUM`,** an older name for the drums track. Write `PART DRUMS`. See [Instrument tracks](/mid/instrument-tracks#deprecated).

## Ignored

- **The `mix` flags `easy` and `easynokick`.** Clone Hero and scan-chart ignore them. YARG reads one as the end of a flip, so an exporter should not write one while a flip is on.
- **`[ENABLE_CHART_DYNAMICS]` in another track.**
- **Notes 24 to 51,** which Rock Band charts use for animations, and YARG reads.
- **Phase Shift SysEx events,** which have no meaning in a drums track.
- **Other drum tracks,** such as `PART DRUMS_2X` and `PART ELITE_DRUMS`. See [Instrument tracks](/mid/instrument-tracks#ignored).

## Invalid forms

<Accordion title="Both five_lane_drums and pro_drums set to true">
A scanner reads the chart as 5-lane.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Overlapping tom markers of one color">
Clone Hero and scan-chart read a tom. YARG switches a note between cymbal and tom once for each marker over it, so a note under two markers is a cymbal there. scan-chart's export writes one marker for each tom.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="95 and 96 at one tick">
Clone Hero merges them into one double kick, and scan-chart keeps the longer, or the double kick when they're the same length. YARG keeps the one whose note-off comes first. scan-chart's export writes one of them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="[ENABLE_CHART_DYNAMICS] after some notes">
Clone Hero and scan-chart give accents and ghosts only to the notes after it. YARG gives them to every note whose note-off comes after it, so a note that starts before the event and ends after it can be an accent there. scan-chart's export writes the event at tick 0, with velocity 127 or 1 only on the notes that had an accent or ghost.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="[ENABLE_CHART_DYNAMICS] spelled another way">
Clone Hero and scan-chart read only the two standard spellings, in a text event. YARG also reads the event with spaces around it, and in other types of text event.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A mix event spelled another way">
Scanners read other spellings differently. Clone Hero reads `[mix  3 drums0d]`, with two spaces, and `[mix 03 drums0d]` as a flip, and `[mix 3 drumsd]` as turning it off. YARG reads `[mix 3 drums 0d]` and `mix3drums0d` as a flip. scan-chart reads only one space or `_` between the parts, and its export writes every mix event it reads as `[mix <difficulty> drums0<flag>]`.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Two mix events of one difficulty at one tick">
Clone Hero and scan-chart use an event that turns the flip off, then `d`, then `dnoflip`, as in a `.chart` file. YARG sorts the events at one tick by their text, so it uses `dnoflip`, then `d`, then an event that turns the flip off. scan-chart's export writes one event.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A dnoflip while a d flip is on">
Released Clone Hero keeps the flip on until an event turns it off. YARG and scan-chart start no-flip at the `dnoflip`. scan-chart's export writes such a pair as it read it, with no event between them.
</Accordion>
