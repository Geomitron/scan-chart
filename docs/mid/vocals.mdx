---
title: "Vocals and harmonies"
description: "Vocal notes, lyrics, phrases and harmonies in a .mid file."
---

A `.mid` file holds its vocals in vocal tracks: `PART VOCALS` for the lead vocals, and `HARM1` to `HARM3` for up to three harmonies. Each holds the notes to sing, the lyric sung on each, and phrases that group them into lines:

```text
PART VOCALS
  0      Track name  PART VOCALS
  1920   Note on     105  Phrase
  1920   Note on     60   Pitch C4
  1920   Lyric       Hel-
  2160   Note off    60
  2160   Note on     62   Pitch D4
  2160   Lyric       lo
  2640   Note off    62
  2880   Note off    105
```

Here one phrase holds two notes: `Hel-` sung on C4, joined to `lo` on D4.

Clone Hero reads only `PART VOCALS`, and only its lyrics and phrases: it shows the lyrics, and has no vocals to sing. YARG reads every vocal track, as vocals and harmonies to sing. A `.chart` file holds one set of lyrics, without notes. See [Lyrics](/chart/events#lyrics).

## Vocal tracks

| Track | Part |
|---|---|
| `PART VOCALS` | Lead vocals, and the lyrics Clone Hero shows |
| `HARM1` | Harmony 1 |
| `HARM2` | Harmony 2 |
| `HARM3` | Harmony 3 |

- Each part must have only one track.
- The harmony tracks must come in order in the file: `HARM1`, then `HARM2`, then `HARM3`.
- A chart with harmonies should also have `PART VOCALS`, because Clone Hero shows lyrics only from `PART VOCALS`.

## Notes

| Note | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 36 to 84 | A note to sing, from C2 to C6. Its number is its pitch |
| 96 | A percussion note, read with no length |
| 105 | A phrase. See [Phrases](#phrases) |
| 116 | Star power. See [Star power](#star-power) |
| 0 | A range shift: YARG works out the pitch range it shows again from this note, for the note's length |
| 1 | A lyric shift: YARG starts a new line of the lyrics it shows, on `PART VOCALS` and `HARM1` |

- A note's length is how long it's sung. Vocal notes have no sustain cutoff.
- Notes of one part must not overlap.
- Clone Hero reads only the phrases. YARG reads every note in the table.

A part has vocals to sing only if it has a note from 36 to 84, or a 96, inside a phrase. YARG and scan-chart list a vocals part only then.

## Lyrics

A lyric is a text event on a vocal track: the syllable sung on the note at its tick.

- **Recommended:** a lyric event, `FF 05`. A text event, `FF 01`, is a lyric too.
- In a part with notes, each lyric must be on a note's tick, and each note should have one lyric. A note that slides from the note before it has the lyric `+`.
- A part must have at most one lyric at a tick.
- A lyric must not be empty, start or end with a space, or hold `[` or `]`.

Unlike in a `.chart` file, a lyric has no `lyric` prefix, and it's in a vocal track, not in `EVENTS`.

### Lyric symbols

A symbol at the end of a lyric changes how it's sung or shown. YARG reads each one as a property of the lyric's note. Clone Hero only changes how it shows the lyric:

| Symbol | Meaning | Clone Hero shows |
|---|---|---|
| `-` | Joined to the next syllable | Nothing, and no space before the next syllable |
| `=` | A hyphen, joined to the next syllable | `-` |
| `+` | A slide from the previous note | Nothing. A lyric of only `+` isn't shown |
| `#` | Not pitched | Nothing |
| `^` | Not pitched, and scored leniently | Nothing |
| `*` | Not pitched | Nothing |
| `%` | A range shift, in YARG | Nothing |
| `/` | A static lyric shift, in YARG | Nothing |
| `$` | Hidden on harmonies 2 and 3 | Nothing |
| `§` | Two syllables sung as one | A space |
| `_` | A space | A space |

- `$` may also come at the start of a lyric.
- A symbol anywhere else in a lyric has no meaning, but isn't shown.
- Clone Hero shows the symbols the same way in a `.chart` file's lyrics.

### Formatting tags

A lyric may hold a formatting tag, such as `<i>` and `</i>` around italic text. Clone Hero allows the tags `i`, `b`, `s`, `u`, `sup`, `sub`, `br`, `voffset`, `lowercase`, `uppercase`, `smallcaps`, `cspace`, `color` and `mspace`, and colors written as `<#rrggbb>`. A lyric must not hold any other text in angle brackets: Clone Hero removes it.

## Phrases

A phrase is a note 105. It holds the notes and lyrics that start from its note-on up to, but not including, its note-off:

- Every note and lyric must be inside a phrase.
- Phrases of one part must not overlap, and a phrase must have a length.
- A lyric on the tick where one phrase ends and the next starts is in the next phrase.

Unlike a `.chart` phrase, a `.mid` phrase is one note, so it always has an end. A lyric on its end tick isn't in it.

{/* review: Q9
Does a phrase hold the lyric on its end tick? In a .chart file it does, unless the next phrase starts there. In a .mid file, Clone Hero puts such a lyric in the ending phrase. YARG's vocals and scan-chart put it in the next phrase, as they do with a note on that tick.
- A. No, the end tick is outside | A phrase holds notes and lyrics up to, but not including, its end tick. A lyric on the end tick is outside every phrase, which is invalid | Stricter than .chart
- B. Yes, as in .chart | A phrase holds lyrics through its end tick, unless the next phrase starts there | YARG can put a lyric and its note on the end tick in different phrases
Recommendation: A. A lyric sits on its note's tick, and YARG and scan-chart leave a note on the end tick out of the phrase, so B can split a lyric from its note.
*/}

**Edge case: lyrics without notes.** A part may hold phrases and lyrics with no notes, for lyrics without pitches. Clone Hero and YARG show its lyrics, and YARG lists no vocals part for it. Clone Hero lists the folder in `badsongs.txt`, with a warning that its lyrics have no notes.

## Star power

Note 116 on `PART VOCALS` or `HARM1` gives star power to each phrase that starts inside it. Clone Hero ignores it. `HARM2` and `HARM3` take `HARM1`'s star power.

## Harmonies

The three harmony parts share their phrases:

- `HARM1`'s phrases are the phrases of all three harmonies: they set where each is scored, and its star power. A `HARM2` or `HARM3` note outside them isn't scored.
- `HARM2`'s phrases mark where YARG moves the lyrics of harmonies 2 and 3 to a new line. They must cover every `HARM3` note.
- `HARM3` has no phrases of its own.

## Deprecated

- **`PART HARM1` to `PART HARM3`,** names from The Beatles: Rock Band. YARG reads them as `HARM1` to `HARM3` when the file has no `HARM1` to `HARM3`, and scan-chart reads them as the same parts. Write `HARM1` to `HARM3` instead. A file must not hold both names of a part.
- **Note 106,** the second player's phrase in older Rock Band charts. Every scanner reads it as a phrase, but they disagree where it differs from a 105. Write 105 instead. A 106 must start and end where a 105 does.
- **`[range_shift]` and `[range_shift <seconds>]`.** YARG reads them as a range shift, and Clone Hero and scan-chart ignore them. Write note 0 instead.

## Ignored

- **Note 97,** hidden percussion. No scanner reads it.
- **Any other note number,** such as 104, which YARG reads as a censorship marker after a `[CENSORSHIP_MARKERS]` event.
- **A text event that starts with `[`,** such as `[play]`. No scanner reads it as a lyric, and scan-chart writes it back as a text event.
- **An empty lyric.**
- **Star power on `HARM2` and `HARM3`, and phrases on `HARM3`.** YARG and scan-chart use `HARM1`'s and `HARM2`'s instead.

## Invalid forms

<Accordion title="A second track for one part">
Clone Hero reads the first `PART VOCALS`. YARG sings the first `PART VOCALS`, and the last of each harmony track. scan-chart reads the first track of each part, and its export leaves out the others.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Both HARMn and PART HARMn for one part">
YARG uses `HARMn`, wherever it is. scan-chart uses whichever comes first in the file, and its export writes it as `HARMn`. Clone Hero reads neither.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Harmony tracks out of order, or without HARM1">
From its code, YARG copies `HARM1`'s phrases into `HARM2` and `HARM3` as it reads each track, so a harmony track before `HARM1` gets no phrases there. Without `HARM1`, YARG doesn't list harmonies 2 and 3. scan-chart reads the tracks in any order, and uses `HARM2`'s and `HARM3`'s own phrases when there's no `HARM1`. Its export writes the tracks in order.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A lyric or note outside every phrase">
Clone Hero doesn't show such a lyric, and YARG doesn't sing such a note. YARG's vocals and scan-chart join a lyric before a phrase to that phrase, and scan-chart drops a lyric after the last phrase. A part with no phrases at all shows nothing in any scanner, and scan-chart's export writes it empty.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A lyric on a phrase's end tick">
Clone Hero shows it in the ending phrase. YARG's vocals and scan-chart put it in the next phrase, and scan-chart drops it after the last phrase.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Overlapping phrases, or a 106 that doesn't match a 105">
Clone Hero shows a lyric inside two phrases in both. scan-chart gives each note and lyric to the first phrase that ends after it. Of a 105 and a 106 that start at one tick, Clone Hero keeps the later in the file, YARG the shorter, and scan-chart the longer, and scan-chart's export writes only that one.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A phrase of length 0">
Clone Hero shows the lyric on its tick in it. YARG's vocals and scan-chart put nothing in it, and give the lyric on its tick to the next phrase. scan-chart's export writes it one tick long, which then holds that lyric.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A lyric in another type of text event">
Clone Hero reads every type of text event as a lyric, except a track name and a copyright notice. YARG reads the instrument name, `FF 04`, the marker, `FF 06`, and the cue point, `FF 07`, as lyrics too. scan-chart reads `FF 06` and `FF 07`, but not `FF 04`. scan-chart's export writes every lyric it reads as `FF 05`.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A bracket inside a lyric">
Clone Hero and scan-chart read `brk[x]` as the lyric `brk[x]`. YARG's vocals read it as the text `x`, not a lyric.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Spaces at a lyric's ends">
Clone Hero shows the spaces, and shows a lyric of only spaces as spaces. YARG's vocals and scan-chart remove them, and scan-chart's export writes the lyric without them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Two lyrics at one tick">
Clone Hero shows both, in the order of the file. YARG and scan-chart keep one when the two are the same, and otherwise sort them. scan-chart's export writes them sorted.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A lyric that isn't on its note's tick">
It has no effect in Clone Hero. YARG and scan-chart give its symbols to the next note, and YARG can leave a lyric that matches no note out of the lines of lyrics it shows.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Overlapping notes of one part">
For two notes of one pitch, YARG and scan-chart ignore a note-on while the pitch is on, and end the first note at the first note-off. This differs from how notes of an instrument track pair. See [Note pairing](/mid/note-pairing). YARG and scan-chart keep both of two overlapping notes of different pitches.
</Accordion>
