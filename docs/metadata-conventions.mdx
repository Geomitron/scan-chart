The [Chorus Encore Application Guidelines](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1XrLKnpL0xawaC90M4lLnhqhExwXM_3JpJnzNqlB_agM) give a style for the values in `song.ini`, so that songs are easy to find and read alike in the song list. Each rule is a recommendation. [Metadata](/ini/metadata) gives what each key means, and [song.ini syntax](/ini/reading-rules) how values are written.

```ini
[song]
name = Song Name (ft. Guest Artist) (Overchart)
artist = Artist
album = Album Name
genre = Progressive Metal
year = 2004
charter = Charter A & Charter B
song_length = 243512
diff_band = 5
diff_guitar = 6
diff_bass = 4
diff_drums = 5
preview_start_time = 62000
preview_end_time = 92000
loading_phrase = Tempo map by Charter C
album_track = 7
```

scan-chart reports a missing `name`, `artist`, `album`, `genre`, `year` or `charter` as `missingValue`, and checks difficulties as [Metadata](/ini/metadata#difficulties) says. It doesn't check the other rules on this page.

## Keys and their order

`song.ini` should hold the keys a song needs, in this order:

`name`, `artist`, `album`, `genre`, `year`, `charter`, `song_length`, `diff_band`, `diff_guitar`, `diff_guitar_coop`, `diff_rhythm`, `diff_bass`, `diff_drums`, `diff_drums_real`, `diff_keys`, `diff_keys_ghl`, `diff_guitarghl`, `diff_guitar_coop_ghl`, `diff_rhythm_ghl`, `diff_bassghl`, `diff_vocals`, `preview_start_time`, `preview_end_time`, `icon`, `loading_phrase`, `album_track`, `playlist_track`, `modchart`, `sustain_cutoff_threshold`, `chord_snap_threshold`, `video_start_time`, `five_lane_drums`, `pro_drums`

- Other keys, such as `explicit_lyrics` and YARG's own, follow them.
- This is the guidelines' order, with the keys they leave out where scan-chart's exporter writes them. The order doesn't change how a scanner reads the file.
- A key that isn't set should be left out, not written blank, as `-1`, or as `False`.

## All text values

- **No abbreviations,** unless the artist uses them. "ft." or "feat.", and "OST", are common exceptions.
- **Title Case:** every word capitalized except minor words, such as "the", "an", "a" and "of".
- **The artist's own styling:** unusual capitalization or styling only where the artist uses it.
- **"and" and "&":** text the artist wrote keeps its "and". Where you join separate names, such as two artists or two charters, use "&": `Charter A & Charter B`.
- **Spacing:** single spaces, and a space on each side of `&`, `/` and each title tag. A title tag is text after the song's name in parentheses or brackets, such as `(Live)`.
- **Romanized names:** a value in a language that's hard to type in English may be written in its romanized form, so that players can search for it. The original may follow it in a title tag: `Gangnam Style (강남스타일)`.

Clone Hero sorts and searches without letter case or style tags, so Title Case and styling change only how a value looks.

### Style tags

Style tags, such as `<color=#AEFFFF>`, should be used only in `charter` and `loading_phrase`. In `name`, `artist`, `album`, `genre` and `year`, the only styling is the artist's own. [Metadata](/ini/metadata#style-tags) says what scanners do with style tags.

## name

- Several songs in one chart: their names separated by ` / `, as in `Song A / Song B`.
- Featured artists: in a title tag that starts with "ft.", or with "feat." when the original title uses it: `Song Name (ft. Guest Artist)`.
- Title tags that are part of the song's name whatever the chart, such as "ft.", a remix or "Full Album", come first.
- Title tags that tell charts of one song apart, such as "Overchart", "6-Fret" or "Open Notes", come after every other title tag.
- Title tags are in parentheses. Brackets are only for a specific reason.

## artist

- Every song should have one: the song's artists, joined with "&".
- A cover: the covering artist, with the covered artist in the song's name.
- A remix: the remixed artist, with the remixing artist in the song's name.

The guidelines don't give a form for these title tags.

## album

- Every song should have one.
- A song with no album: the song's name, followed by ` - (Single)`: `Song Name - (Single)`.
- A live recording: the place where it was performed.
- A song on several albums: any of them, but the audio and the album art should match the album named.

## genre

- Every song should have one genre, as specific as possible: "Complextro" is better than "Electronic".

## year

- Every song should have one year, without a month, a day or a time.
- The year the album named in `album` was released. For a live recording, the year of the performance.

## charter

- Every song should have one: the names of everyone who placed notes in the chart, joined with "&".
- Credit for other work, such as reviewing note placement, adding lyrics, tempo mapping or preparing the audio, may go in `loading_phrase` instead.
- When charters split the work by instrument or difficulty, each name may be followed by its part in parentheses: `Charter A (Guitar) & Charter B (Keys)`.
- Credit goes in `charter`, not `frets`: see [Metadata](/ini/metadata#deprecated).

## song_length

Every song should have one: the length of its longest audio file, in milliseconds. [Metadata](/ini/metadata#the-song-list) says which files count.

## Difficulties

- Every instrument the chart has should have its difficulty, from 0 to 6, or up to 10 for a very difficult chart.
- `diff_band` gives the difficulty of the whole song.

## The song list

- **`preview_start_time` and `preview_end_time`:** where the song list's preview starts and ends.
- **`icon`:** the icon's name, without its extension: `icon = mysetlist`, not `mysetlist.png`. Icon names hold only lowercase letters, digits, `-` and `_`. Leave `icon` out when it would be the charter's name in lowercase, since Clone Hero already looks for that icon. Icons come with the game, not with a chart: the [list of icons](https://clonehero.gitlab.io/sources/) gives their names.
- **`loading_phrase`:** a phrase shown before the chart begins. It may also hold credits, as above.
- **`album_track`:** the song's position on its album, counting from 1. Leave it out for a single, and don't write `0` or `16000`.
- **`playlist_track`:** the chart's position in its setlist, counting from 1, and only when it's in one. A setlist is any group of charts meant to be downloaded as one unit, such as all the charts of an official game, a chart pack, a full album or a discography.
- **`modchart`:** `True` for a modchart, and left out otherwise. The guidelines don't define a modchart. Clone Hero uses the key only to filter the song list.
- **`explicit_lyrics`:** `True` when the lyrics are explicit, and left out otherwise. Only Clone Hero reads it.

The guidelines' rules for `video_start_time`, `five_lane_drums` and `pro_drums` match [Settings](/ini/settings).

## Differences from the guidelines

- **Forbidden keys.** The guidelines forbid `delay`, `hopo_frequency`, `eighthnote_hopo`, `multiplier_note`, `frets`, `track` and `end_events`. Games still read all but `end_events`, and older charts depend on them, so this specification deprecates them: a chart that holds them is valid, and [Settings](/ini/settings#deprecated) says what to change before removing each. `end_events` does nothing, and is ignored. `multiplier_note = 116` is still needed in one case.
- **`frets` as a credit.** The guidelines' credit rule allows `charter` or `frets`, and their key list forbids `frets`. This specification deprecates `frets`: write `charter`.
- **The key list.** The guidelines allow only the keys in their list. This specification also has `preview_end_time`, `explicit_lyrics`, `diff_vocals`, `diff_keys_ghl`, `diff_guitar_coop_ghl`, `diff_rhythm_ghl`, `playlist`, `sub_playlist`, `sustain_cutoff_threshold` and `chord_snap_threshold`, which games read, and allows the keys only YARG reads.
- **`-1`.** The guidelines say no value is `-1`. Here `-1` is valid in difficulties and preview times, and means not set, though a song should still leave the key out.
